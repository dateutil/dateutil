Fixed `tz.tzstr` raising `TypeError: unsupported operand type(s) for *=: "NoneType" and "int"` for TZ strings naming `GMT` or `UTC` with no offset, such as `tzstr("GMT")` or `gettz("GMT,4;3/4")`. These now behave like any other offset-less abbreviation and produce a zero offset. Reported by @sunhuachuang and @danyeaw (gh issue #1432, gh issue #1402). Fixed by @<submitter> (gh pr #<PR>)
