Fixed ``rruleset.rdate`` and ``rruleset.exdate`` with :py:class:`datetime.date`
values. They are now converted to a :py:class:`datetime.datetime` at midnight,
as for a date-only ``dtstart``. Before, iteration could raise ``TypeError``.

@hark-agent (gh pr #1607)
