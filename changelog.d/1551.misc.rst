Fixed the bounds in `tests/property/test_tz_prop.py` being derived from `datetime.fromtimestamp()`, which returns local wall times while the hypothesis strategy generates UTC datetimes; in any zone with a non-zero UTC offset this let the strategy leave the range representable in the 32-bit block of a `TZif` file, failing the suite. Also registered the `no_cover` pytest marker that `tests/conftest.py` applies, without which a plain `pytest tests` run aborts during collection, and renamed `changelog.d/1463.docs.rst` to the `doc` fragment type declared in `pyproject.toml`.
